Our Mission & Vision

Founded in Butwal-10 by Dr. Siddhartha Vishvakarma and Dr. Jatin Belbase, Siddhartha STEM College was established with a clear purpose: to make world-class STEM education easily accessible to local students in Butwal. We nurture young minds from foundational primary science all the way to specialized global engineering degrees under one roof.
